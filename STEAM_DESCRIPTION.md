## About This Game (BBCode — вставлять в Steamworks как есть)

```
[h1]Find. Grow stronger. Go deeper.[/h1]

[i]Immersion[/i] is a couch-friendly action roguelike with a top-down view. Every run is a descent through procedurally generated floors of a living dungeon: monsters patrol, call their allies for help, flee from you at low health and try to flank you — and every chapter hits harder than the last. Your strength is what you manage to find. And everything you earn — points, gear, progress — stays with you between runs.

[h1]Two heroes, one screen[/h1]

Local co-op with no network, accounts or lobbies: the second player joins in ten seconds on the same keyboard and/or a pair of gamepads. Each hero keeps a personal inventory, level and skill tree, while the shared auto-zooming camera keeps both of you in the action.

[h1]Four heroes, four playstyles[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Rogue[/b] — fragile but elusive: quick precise stabs, high evasion, poisoned weapon, stealth and a trail of spikes.[/*]
[*][b]Sorceress[/b] — a ranged mage with the highest wisdom and the lowest health: fireballs, freezing frost and a stone spike that scatters crowds.[/*]
[*][b]Knight[/b] — a walking fortress: heavy blows, super defense, a retribution aura that wounds attackers and a damage cap that cuts anything above a threshold.[/*]
[*][b]Valkyrie[/b] — speed and reach: a piercing dash through enemies, acceleration for running and a war dance that fires a dart on every third attack.[/*]
[/list]

[h1]A build made of loot[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]13 equipment slots[/b] and [b]4 rarity tiers[/b] with 15 magical prefixes.[/*]
[*][b]5 legendary sets[/b] with 2/4/6-piece bonuses that change how you play.[/*]
[*][b]9 unique "Eternal" relics[/b] that bend the rules of the entire run.[/*]
[*][b]Alchemy[/b]: merge three items into one of a higher rank.[/*]
[*]Epic and legendary gear grants [b]active abilities[/b]; food keeps you alive between fights.[/*]
[/list]

[h1]A dungeon that fights back[/h1]

29 enemy types with honest AI instead of scripted dummies — they patrol, raise the alarm, retreat when wounded and circle around to attack from the sides. Six bosses with authored mechanics: the Goblin Chieftain's rage, the Spider Boss's acid rain, the Demon's summoning ritual with four pillars to shatter. And at the end of the final chapter you choose which of the three Void bosses to face: the Void Cyclops, the Void Medusa or the Void Mushroom.

[h1]Every failure is a step forward[/h1]

Points from every run feed [b]7 upgrade branches[/b] in your lobby: more chest capacity, better starting gear, more keys, more health and more. A [b]meta chest[/b] lets you stash loot and bring it into your next run. A bestiary fills up as you fight, and 12 achievements reward the ways you play, not just the way you win.

[h1]Quests with a choice[/h1]

Every meeting with an NPC is a fork in the road: escort the allied Wolf or turn it away; fight the Fire Elemental or chase it across the lava; out-trick the barbarian Hrup in a race for treasure — or force him to fight you for it. Your decisions change how the run plays out.

[h1]Made for short evenings[/h1]

A chapter run takes 15–40 minutes. Progress is saved locally, and a save file can even switch a run between solo and co-op. Fully localized in English and Russian.

Grab a friend — the dungeon is already waiting.
```
